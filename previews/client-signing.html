Card step states fill Visa's test number and press the button on their own, so the result shows. Reply lost: the button stays on "Confirming your payment" and the QuickBooks-page link and More options switch off, so a customer whose card may already be charged cannot pick a second way to pay. Card saved, charge pending is what shows if Intuit was down at the moment of the charge: the card is on file, and Charges to run lists the charge under Needs attention.

The signed PDF

What lands in the company Drive and the customer's inbox, shown as letter pages: the agreement, both signatures on matching lines, the record fingerprint, then the Notice of Cancellation on its own page. A test agreement adds a red TEST AGREEMENT bar at the top. Google makes the real PDF; run pdfSample in the script editor to see Google's own output in admin@'s Drive.
