Sale Process Map

Every step from a lead to money in QuickBooks: who acts, what runs on its own, the checks (gates) that can stop a step, the records written, and the emails sent. Read from the code that is about to go live: Code.gs with card mode on, and CRM and signing page commits c1ba2b4 through af91d5c. Anything not built yet is marked and listed at the end.

The phone sale at a glance

A person does it Runs on its own Has a gate that can stop it

Columns are the order of events. Steps 4 to 7 happen while the customer is on the page, usually inside five minutes. The in-person path and later charges are below.

Each path, step by step

  1. Rep · CRM

    Quick add the lead, then Add and quote

    What happens
    Name, phone, address, email, region and source are saved on a new lead (stage New). The Seasonal Services Agreement setup opens with the email filled in and the pricing area taken from the region.
    Gates
    A phone number already in the CRM is flagged while typing.
    Writes
    clients/{id}, phoneIndex
  2. Rep · CRM

    Build the quote

    What happens
    Tick Fall Cleanup, Christmas Lights, Snow Removal; add-ons; neighbor credit. Charged today updates live: fall and snow in full, half the lights price. The other half waits until the lights are marked installed.
    Gates
    • Service property and at least one service
    • Westland: no Fall Cleanup and no Showcase
    • Lights need a package and a color; snow needs a lot type
    • Walked price for turf over 10,000 sq ft
    • Neighbor name and address when the credit is ticked
    • Valid email addresses
    Writes
    Draft on the client (ssaDraft) when saved for later.
  3. Rep · CRM

    Email contract to sign

    What happens
    The CRM writes the link: the quote, the agreement text with the signing date left blank, the Notice of Cancellation, and the client changes to apply when signed. An older open link for the same client is cancelled. A lead moves to Quoted. The sent screen shows the phone script and live status.
    Gates
    • Customer email required
    • Signing service set up (More, Contracts, Signing service)
    • Rep's countersignature saved once
    Writes
    sync/sign/{token} (expires in 7 days), clients/{id}/signReq, client log
    Sends
    Asks the signing service to send now.
  4. Signing service · Gmail

    The quote email goes out

    What happens
    One email from admin@ with the quote table, Total, Charged today, the snow note, and one button: Review, sign, and add your card. Status becomes Sent, then Opened when the customer opens the link.
    Gates
    Only a queued link is sent, so a double tap never sends twice. If Gmail refuses, the link shows Email failed and the 5-minute job retries.
  5. Customer · signing page

    Read and sign

    What happens
    Quote, full agreement, Notice of Cancellation. The customer ticks the consent box and types their full name. The date is the day they sign.
    Gates
    • Link exists, is not cancelled, not expired (7 days), not already signed
    • Consent box ticked; typed name of at least 3 characters with a letter
    • The client still exists in the CRM
    • Test agreement rules hold (see the test tab)
  6. Signing service

    Contract locked, client updated

    What happens
    The signed record is written once and can never be edited, only voided. It holds the full text, typed signature, both emails, sent and opened times, device and a SHA-256 fingerprint. The client gets the services at the agreement prices, moves to Active, and is marked payment method missing unless one is already on file. The charges on the schedule are created.
    Writes
    contracts/{id}/{cid}, clients/{id} (services, status, payOnFile, contracts mirror), charge rows in sync/qb: due at signing (ready) and lights balance (waits until the lights service is ticked Installed), client log
    Sends
    PDF saved to admin@'s Drive, folder "Mangiapane Brothers Signed Contracts", one subfolder per client. Signed copy emailed with the PDF attached. If either fails it is queued and retried every 5 minutes.
  7. Customer · Intuit

    Enter the card

    What happens
    Name, number, expiration, security code, ZIP. The browser sends the card straight to Intuit and gets back a one-time token. Only the token comes to us. Bank account and check are under More options.
    Gates
    • Number passes the card check digit; MM/YY; 3 or 4 digit code; ZIP
    • Intuit accepts the card, or the customer sees Intuit's reason and nothing is sent to us
    • If the browser cannot reach Intuit, the page offers the emailed QuickBooks page instead
  8. Signing service · QuickBooks Payments

    Card saved, today's amount charged

    What happens
    The customer is found or created in QuickBooks, the card is saved on them, and the charge due at signing runs on the spot. The CRM stores the card brand, last 4 and expiration only.
    Gates
    • Card or bank numbers are refused outright
    • A card already saved on this link is never saved or charged again
    • 3 declines on one link close the card form for that link
    • reCAPTCHA must say a person (score 0.5 or higher, our site, the card step), or nothing reaches Intuit
    • Charge limits: above $0, at most $3,000 per charge, a saved card for this QuickBooks company, once per charge
    Writes
    payOnFile (on file, brand, last 4, QuickBooks card id), charge row done with the transaction id, payStatus paid or open, client log
    Sends
    Payment receipt from admin@ with the Intuit statement. On a decline: the card is removed, the customer is told nothing was charged, and can try another card.
  9. Customer · Rep

    Confirmation, and the CRM shows it

    What happens
    The page says plainly whether today's amount was charged or is still processing, with the cancel-by date. If the reply was lost, the page waits up to 45 seconds and reads the real result back. It never asks for the card again. The rep's sent screen and the lead card show Signed, the card on file, and today's charge.
    Gates
    Once a card is saved, or while one is being confirmed, no other payment method can be chosen on that link.
  1. Rep · CRM

    Build the quote, then Sign here on this device

    What happens
    Same setup and gates as the phone sale. The agreement shows on the iPad with a signature box.
  2. Customer · Rep

    Customer draws a signature; rep taps Sign and lock

    Gates
    Signature box not empty; the client has a name; company signature drawn once (then automatic).
    What happens
    Contract locked with a fingerprint, client updated and charges created, the same as the emailed path. A QuickBooks customer row is queued for Claude. An open emailed link for that client is cancelled.
    Sends
    The signing service saves the PDF to Drive and emails the signed copy.
  3. Rep · CRM

    Payment on file sheet

    What happens
    Four choices:
    • Scan the card now (GoPayment). Charge in GoPayment, then record brand and last 4 and tick "charged". The signing charge is marked done and a GoPayment match is queued for Claude.
    • Email a QuickBooks card link. A pasted link is emailed from admin@ at once; with no link pasted, a payment-link row is queued for Claude to make and send.
    • Bank account (ACH), under More options: same as the link.
    • Prepay everything by check, under More options: every charge waits for the check; "I have the check now" marks them paid and queues the payment to record.
    Gates
    Last 4 digits only (the box cuts anything longer); brand required; a link must start with https://; email required for a link.
  1. CRM · Tools · Charges to run

    Every charge on the schedule is listed

    Sections
    • Needs attention: a charge that failed, was held, or is running, with the reason
    • Due now: ready to charge, or declined
    • Waiting for the job to be done: the lights balance, the landscaping balance, the second test $1. Each says what opens it; Open now is the override when the work is done and nobody marked it
    • Waiting for a prepay check
    • Charged through QuickBooks Payments, last 45 days
    Totals
    Due-now totals leave out test charges.
  2. Rep · Signing service

    Charge card on file (card saved on the signing page)

    What happens
    Two taps. The CRM writes a one-time request on the charge and the service charges the saved card once, then emails the receipt. Rows waiting for the job to be done are not charged from here until their gate opens.
    Gates
    • Only signed-in admins can write the request; it works once, within 10 minutes
    • The same charge limits as at signing
    • Unknown outcome (Intuit down): marked Needs attention; Try again reuses the same request id, so it cannot charge twice
    • Declined: the client is past due as of today
  3. Rep

    GoPayment cards: charge by hand

    What happens
    Charge in QuickBooks (Customers, the client, Receive payment), then tap Charged or Declined. The first successful one sends the customer "your payment method is confirmed".
  4. Rep · Signing service

    Refund

    What happens
    Two taps on any charge made through QuickBooks Payments in the last 45 days. Same day and nothing refunded yet: the charge is voided and never posts. Otherwise: everything left on the charge is refunded. Partial refunds are done in QuickBooks.
    Sends
    "Charge cancelled" or "Refund receipt" from admin@.
  5. Rep · CRM · the client

    The job-done gate: lights installed, landscaping done

    What happens
    Tick Installed, all lit on the client's lights service and save: the install-day balance moves to Due now (the install date fills in if blank). Set a landscaping job's Status to Done and save: its balance moves to Due now. Then Charge card on file, or Charged in QuickBooks for a GoPayment card.
    Gates
    • Only the change to Installed or Done opens it, once
    • Kept off Undo, so an undo can never put a charged balance back to waiting
    • The employee portal will set the same Installed and Done fields when it is built; the signing service will then open the gate on its 5-minute run, so it works with the CRM closed
  6. Rep · CRM · the client

    Landscaping 50/50

    What happens
    On a saved landscaping job with a price, Bill 50/50 (two taps) adds half as a deposit, due now, and the balance, waiting for Done. The job card shows both and their state.
    Open
    There is no landscaping agreement yet. A deposit is charged with no signed contract until one is written.
  7. Rep · CRM · the client

    Add a charge

    What happens
    On any client: Snow visit past 16 (number of visits; the signed per-visit price fills in), Lights service call ($40 no ladder, $65 ladder), or Something else (what it is for is required). The receipt line is shown before saving. Add to Charges to run, or Add and charge the saved card (two taps).
    Gates
    • Amount above zero and at most $3,000 (the signing service also holds anything over its cap)
    • Date of the work required
    • The CRM does not count snow visits: the rep decides a visit is past 16
  1. Rep · CRM

    Tick Test agreement in the setup

    What happens
    Services, property fields and Sign here are hidden. Charged today shows $1. Email contract sends a TEST- agreement: $1 at signing, a second $1 later, no service. The client's services and status are not touched. The box is never saved in a draft.
  2. Customer

    The same email, page, card form, PDF and receipts

    What happens
    A red TEST AGREEMENT banner on every step, "TEST:" on every email subject, a red bar on the PDF, a TEST tag in Charges to run.
  3. Signing service

    Test guards

    Gates
    • The test flag and the TEST- number must agree
    • At most $1 per charge and $2 per agreement; every charge flagged test
    • Cannot change the client's services or status
    • A real agreement carrying a test line is refused
    • A test charge over $1 is held before it reaches Intuit
  4. Rep

    Charge the second $1, then Refund both

    What happens
    Charges to run, Charge the second $1 now. Then Refund on each: same day, both are voided.
  1. Every 5 minutes

    Queue job

    What happens
    Sends any link still queued, retries queued emails and Drive saves (up to 5 tries, then marked error), expires links older than 7 days, clears sent-email records after 30 days.
  2. Every day, 3 a.m.

    QuickBooks connection refresh

    What happens
    Renews the connection so it never ages out. Tokens are encrypted and never leave the script.
    Gates
    If QuickBooks refuses, charges stop, the CRM status shows reconnect needed, and admin@ gets an email, at most every 12 hours. Reconnect from the script editor (qbConnectLink). Only the Mangiapane Brothers company can connect.
  3. On request

    QuickBooks queue for Claude

    What happens
    Tools, Copy queue for Claude. Claude works it with the QuickBooks connector and the results are pasted back. Nothing runs it on a schedule yet.

Parties and pieces

People

  • Customer: no login. Email, signing page, receipts.
  • Rep or admin: the CRM on iPad, phone or computer. Only admins on the Team list see it.
  • Accountant: reads QuickBooks.
  • Claude: works the QuickBooks queue on request. Never charges a card; the new rules make that impossible for its own sign-in.

Our pieces

  • CRM: apps.mangbrothers.com/crm.html, data in Firebase.
  • Signing page: apps.mangbrothers.com/sign.html, opened from the email.
  • Signing service: Apps Script "MB Signing" running as admin@. Sends mail, saves PDFs, charges cards.

Outside services

  • Intuit, QuickBooks Payments: card tokens, saved cards, charges, refunds.
  • QuickBooks Online: customers and the books.
  • Google: Gmail and Drive on admin@, reCAPTCHA on the card step.
  • GoPayment: in-person cards.
