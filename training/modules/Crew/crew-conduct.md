## You are the company on that property
- The client sees you, not the office. {reveal}
- Our tagline is Full-Service Property Maintenance for Those Who Take Pride in Their Home. Our clients take pride in their homes. Treat every yard that way. {reveal}
- This module and the handbook set the rules. The quiz covers both. {reveal}
- You must pass this quiz before you can be scheduled. {reveal}

---slide---
## Uniform
- Wear the uniform as issued. {reveal}
- No cutting off sleeves or altering it. {reveal}
- Long sleeves or a hoodie underneath are fine. {reveal}
![Crew member in the issued uniform](image:uniform-as-issued)

---slide---
## Language and smoking
- No swearing on the job. {reveal}
- No smoking, vaping or tobacco during work. {reveal}
- Smoking, vaping or tobacco only on lunch, away from client property and the company truck. {reveal}

---slide---
## Phones
- No phones during a job. {reveal}
- In the truck between jobs, music and quick texts are fine. {reveal}
- When the truck stops, put the phone away and get out ready to work. {reveal}
- No phone calls during a job except a true emergency. Take calls in the truck or on lunch. {reveal}
- The navigator uses his phone in the truck to get to the next house. That is part of the job. {reveal}

---slide---
## Music on a property
- Headphones or earbuds only. {reveal}
- Never a speaker on a client's property. {reveal}
- Keep it low enough to hear your manager. {reveal}

---slide---
## Stay where you belong
- Work only the areas the service covers. {reveal}
- Do not go into backyards, garages or sheds unless the job calls for it. {reveal}
- Close any gate you open. {reveal}
- Leave the property cleaner than you found it. {reveal}

---slide---
## When a client talks to you
- Be polite and brief. {reveal}
- Small requests are fine to handle yourself, like "can you grab that." {reveal}
- Anything beyond that, get your manager. {reveal}
- Never promise extra work, a price, a refund or a discount. {reveal}

---slide---
## When a client complains
- Do not argue. Do not explain why it is not your fault. {reveal}
- Say you will get your manager, and get him. {reveal}
- The manager decides whether to redo work on the spot. {reveal}

---slide---
## Damage
- Report any damage to your manager immediately. {reveal}
- Never hide it or leave it for the client to find. {reveal}
- Serious offenses or gross negligence are grounds for firing. {reveal}

---slide---
## Lunch and the truck
- Your manager calls lunch. It can run up to an hour. {reveal}
- The crew stays together. Bring your lunch when you can. {reveal}
- No driving off, and no personal use of the company truck. {reveal}

---slide---
## Read the handbook
- The handbook holds the attendance policy: personal days, notice and unexcused absences. {reveal}
- Read it in full. At least two quiz questions come from it. {reveal}

### Q6

- [x] As two unexcused absences

### Q8
You wake up sick at 5:30 a.m. on a workday. What do you do?
- [ ] Text your manager before the meet time
- [x] Call the operations manager by 6:30 a.m.
- [ ] Skip the day and explain when you come back
- [ ] Call whenever you are up to it
explain: If you know the night before, call that night. If it hits that morning, call the operations manager by 6:30 a.m. A text alone does not count. A late call counts as one unexcused absence, and no call and no show counts as two.
