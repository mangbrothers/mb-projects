## The backpack blower
- The backpack blower is the main tool of the fall season. {reveal}
- It pushes leaves off lawns, out of beds and into the street. {reveal}
- This module covers blowers only. Other equipment is taught in person. {reveal}
![Backpack blower](image:backpack-blower)

---slide---
## Gear on before it starts
- Safety glasses on. {reveal}
- Ear protection on. {reveal}
- Steel-toed boots on. {reveal}
- No exceptions, even for a quick pass. {reveal}

---slide---
## Putting it on
- Both shoulder straps on. Adjust them so the blower sits snug against your back. {reveal}
- Keep the tube and throttle on the side your manager shows you. {reveal}

---slide---
## Starting the PB blower
- Throttle lever to idle, one notch off stop. Choke on for a cold start. {reveal}
- Pump the purge bulb until fuel shows, then 4 or 5 more times. {reveal}
- Blower on flat ground. Pull gently, then fast, up to 5 pulls until it fires. {reveal}
- Choke off and pull until it runs. If it will not start, repeat from the choke. {reveal}
- Let it idle 2 to 3 minutes before you put it on. {reveal}
note: From the Echo PB-9010T manual. Confirm the PB-8010 matches.

---slide---
## Where you point it
- Never at a person, a pet or a car. {reveal}
- Never at an open window or door. {reveal}
- Watch for rocks and mulch. Blown stones break windows and dent cars. {reveal}
- When someone walks up, let off the throttle and turn toward them. {reveal}

---slide---
## Speed for the surface
- Full power on open lawn. {reveal}
- Low power in mulch or rock beds so the bed stays in the bed. {reveal}
- Low power near cars, windows and siding. {reveal}
![Blower on low in a mulch bed](image:blower-low-in-bed)

---slide---
## Fuel
- Fueling happens at the end of the day, not in the morning. {reveal}
- Never refuel a running or hot blower. {reveal}
- Echo PB blowers run on mixed fuel: 50 to 1, gas to two-stroke oil. {reveal}
- Never put straight gas in a blower. It ruins the engine. {reveal}

---slide---
## Backups ride on every truck
- Each crew carries two blowers. {reveal}
- If one dies, switch to the backup and keep working. {reveal}
- The crew finishes the day short-handed rather than pushing houses to Saturday. {reveal}

---slide---
## When a blower breaks
- Tell your manager. {reveal}
- Swap to the backup first. Repairs happen later. {reveal}
- Only the manager attempts a repair, during downtime or at his option over lunch. {reveal}
- Any repair attempt is capped at 20 minutes. {reveal}

---slide---
## End of the day
- The manager handles fueling and checks at the end of the day. {reveal}
- That way the crew arrives at the first house ready to work. {reveal}
- Tell your manager about anything that ran rough, so it gets looked at. {reveal}
