---slide---
## The job in one line
- Get the leaves off the lawn and into the street for city pickup. {reveal}
- Everything else in this module supports that. {reveal}
![Lawn cleared, leaves in the street](image:fall-cleanup-finished)

---slide---
## Why the timing matters
- Cleanups are scheduled the week before the city collects leaves in that area. {reveal}
- The city picks up the piles from the street. {reveal}
- That is why every leaf goes to the street, not into bags or piles in the yard. {reveal}

---slide---
## The lawn is the core job
- Every leaf off the lawn. {reveal}
- Front, sides and back, as far as the service covers. {reveal}
- Backpack blowers push the leaves toward the street. {reveal}

---slide---
## Beds
- Clear leaves out of beds as well as you can. {reveal}
- Beds are not a hard requirement. The lawn is. {reveal}
- Mulch and lava rock beds are not our responsibility. {reveal}
- Blower on low in any mulch or rock bed. {reveal}
![Bed cleared with the blower on low](image:fall-bed-cleared)

---slide---
## Where the pile goes
- All leaves go into the street at the curb. {reveal}
- Keep piles off the driveway. {reveal}
- Keep piles away from the mailbox. {reveal}
- Do not blow leaves into storm drains. {reveal}
![Street pile clear of driveway and mailbox](image:fall-street-pile)

---slide---
## Gutters on a fall cleanup
- Standard gutter leaves are blown out into the yard. {reveal}
- Those leaves are cleared with the rest of the cleanup. {reveal}
- The full gutter rules are in the Gutters module. {reveal}

---slide---
## Before the truck moves
- No leaves left on the lawn. {reveal}
- Driveway and mailbox clear of piles. {reveal}
- Walks and porch blown clean. {reveal}
- Your manager checks the house before checking it off. {reveal}

---slide---
## What you never do
- Pile leaves on a neighbor's frontage. {reveal}
- Leave piles on the sidewalk or driveway. {reveal}
- Promise a client extra work. Get your manager. {reveal}
