---slide---
## Safety comes before the route
- Nobody gets hurt to finish a house. {reveal}
- If something feels unsafe, stop and tell your manager. {reveal}
- Your manager covers the day's biggest risk in the morning brief. Listen to it. {reveal}
- You must pass this quiz before you can be scheduled. {reveal}

---slide---
## What you bring
- Steel-toed boots. Required on every job. You supply your own. {reveal}
- Clothing for the weather: layers, rain gear, cold-weather gear. {reveal}
- Gloves are not required, but you may wear your own. {reveal}
![Steel-toed work boots](image:steel-toed-boots)

---slide---
## What the company provides
- Safety glasses. {reveal}
- Ear protection: sound-canceling headphones. {reveal}
- Wear both any time a blower or other power equipment is running. {reveal}
![Company safety glasses and headphones](image:company-safety-gear)

---slide---
## Ear protection is mandatory
- Some ear protection is required whenever equipment is running. {reveal}
- You may wear earbuds under the company headphones. {reveal}
- You may wear your own headphones only if they are NRR-rated hearing protection. {reveal}
- If yours are not NRR-rated, wear the company headphones. {reveal}

---slide---
## Music
- Music only through headphones or earbuds. {reveal}
- Never through a speaker on a client's property. {reveal}
- Keep the volume low enough to hear your manager and traffic. {reveal}

---slide---
## Ladders and roofs
- No one goes on a wet, frosty or icy roof. {reveal}
- The ladder is held or spotted while anyone climbs. {reveal}
- The ladder extends three feet above the roof edge. {reveal}
- The manager makes the final call on steep roofs. {reveal}
- You may refuse a roof you think is unsafe, with no penalty. {reveal}
![Ladder extending three feet above the roof edge](image:ladder-three-feet)

---slide---
## The truck and trailer
- Only the crew manager drives the truck and trailer. {reveal}
- No personal use of the company truck, ever. {reveal}
- Stay clear of the trailer while it is being loaded, unloaded or hitched. {reveal}

---slide---
## If someone is hurt: life-threatening
- Call 911. Do not drive him yourself. {reveal}
- Life-threatening means a roof fall, a head injury or heavy bleeding. {reveal}
- Stay with him until help arrives. {reveal}
- Another crew member calls Russel. {reveal}

---slide---
## If someone is hurt: serious, not life-threatening
- Get him to the closest hospital first. {reveal}
- Another crew member calls Russel on the way. {reveal}
- Reporting happens after he is being taken care of. {reveal}
- Closest hospital: Trinity Health Livonia Hospital, 36475 Five Mile Rd at Levan. {reveal}

---slide---
## Minor injuries
- Minor cuts, sprains and similar injuries go to urgent care. {reveal}
- Tell your manager right away, even if it seems small. {reveal}
- Your manager reports it the same day. {reveal}

---slide---
## Damage on a property
- Accidents happen. Hiding one is what gets you fired. {reveal}
- Report any damage to your manager immediately. {reveal}
- Never hide it, cover it or leave it for the client to find. {reveal}
- Gross negligence is grounds for firing. {reveal}
