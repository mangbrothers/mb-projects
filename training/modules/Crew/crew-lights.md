## What we install
- Roofline: C9 socket wire with bulbs, clipped to the gutter. {reveal}
- Shrubs and trees: 5mm light strings, wrapped. {reveal}
- A wreath on the front door of every job. {reveal}
- No net lights and no garland. {reveal}
![Finished roofline, shrubs and wreath](image:lights-finished-house)

---slide---
## Prepped before the season
- Bulbs and clips are already on the wire before install day. {reveal}
- Warm white and multicolor. Check the work order for which one. {reveal}
- Hardware is green, black or bronze only. Never orange. {reveal}

---slide---
## Two-person roofline
- One person is on the roof or ladder. One is on the ground. {reveal}
- The ground person feeds wire from the spool. {reveal}
- The roof person only clips the wire into the gutter as he goes. {reveal}
- All roof and ladder rules from the Safety module apply. {reveal}
![Ground person feeding wire from the spool](image:lights-spool-feed)

---slide---
## Nothing gets cut on the roof
- No cutting wire on the roof. {reveal}
- No extension cords run up to the roofline. {reveal}
- Extra wire runs down the side of the house with the bulbs removed. {reveal}
- Every cut and every connection happens at ground level. {reveal}

---slide---
## One continuous run
- One run of wire goes from the roofline down to the bushes and most trees. {reveal}
- Where a gap is wanted, leave the socket empty. {reveal}
- Every empty socket gets a cap. {reveal}
- No splitters and no second cord. {reveal}

---slide---
## Cutting and plugs
- Cut the wire on site as you go. {reveal}
- Add a slide-on plug at the cut. Steps are on the next slide. {reveal}
- Use only the Novelty Lights SPT-1 plugs that match the wire. {reveal}

---slide---
## Attaching a slide-on plug
- Cut the wire straight across. {reveal}
- Pull the back cover off the plug. {reveal}
- Lay the wire end in, ribbed side of the wire on the wide-prong side. {reveal}
- Slide the cover back on until it clicks. The prongs pierce the wire. {reveal}
- Tug to check it is seated, then test the run before you hang it. {reveal}

---slide---
## Shrubs and trees
- Standard shrub: 2 strings, wrapped. {reveal}
- Large shrub: 4 strings, wrapped. {reveal}
- Medium tree: one ladder high, the trunk and a couple of offshoots, 8 strings. {reveal}
![Shrub wrapped with 5mm strings](image:lights-shrub-wrap)

---slide---
## Cords, joints and timer
- Use the store-length green cords. {reveal}
- Every cord joint gets a Twist and Seal cover. No bags. {reveal}
- Every job gets the photocell timer at the outlet. {reveal}
- If an outlet trips, tell your manager. It is handled case by case. {reveal}

---slide---
## The wreath
- One wreath on every job. {reveal}
- Steel door: magnetic hanger. {reveal}
- Every other door: padded over-the-door hanger. {reveal}
- Never Command hooks. {reveal}

---slide---
## Before you leave
- Every bulb lit. Test before the truck moves. {reveal}
- No cords across walkways. {reveal}
- Nothing left on the roof or lawn. {reveal}
