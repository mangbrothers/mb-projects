---slide---
## Snow does not follow a schedule
- Crews go out when it snows, not on a fixed weekly schedule. {reveal}
- The operations manager makes the go or no-go call. {reveal}
- Our promise to clients is that snow is cleared within 24 hours. {reveal}

---slide---
## When crews go out
- 1 inch or more: crews go out once the storm stops. {reveal}
- 5 inches or more: the operations manager may send crews mid-storm. {reveal}
- A mid-storm trip is a second push. Crews go out again after the storm. {reveal}
note: Pay for each push is in the crew agreement, not here.

---slide---
## The night-before alert
- When snow is likely overnight, a heads-up text goes out the night before. {reveal}
- Be up by 6:00 a.m. to check the go or no-go text. {reveal}
- A go means a 7:00 a.m. start. {reveal}

---slide---
## Daytime storms
- Before a daytime storm, crews get a 48-hour standby alert. {reveal}
- About two hours before the storm is forecast to end, the operations manager texts a meet time and place. {reveal}
- The meet time can be pushed back if the storm runs long. {reveal}

---slide---
## Confirm every alert
- Reply "confirmed" to the group text within 30 minutes. {reveal}
- No reply means a call from the operations manager. {reveal}
- Confirming tells the operations manager the crew is covered. {reveal}

---slide---
## Snow hours
- No snow work between 11:00 p.m. and 7:00 a.m. {reveal}
- Alerts may come overnight so you are ready for a 7:00 a.m. start. {reveal}

---slide---
## What gets cleared at every house
- The driveway, up to the back gate. Never the backyard. {reveal}
- The front porch. {reveal}
- The walk to the porch. {reveal}
- The public sidewalk. {reveal}
![Driveway cleared to the back gate](image:snow-driveway-scope)

---slide---
## Where the snow goes
- Push snow to the sides of the driveway, near the street. {reveal}
- That is where the city plow piles snow anyway. {reveal}
- Never onto the walk you just cleared or onto the neighbor's driveway. {reveal}

---slide---
## Salt every visit
- Salt after every visit. {reveal}
- Use calcium chloride ice melt only. Never rock salt. {reveal}
- Cover the driveway, walk, porch steps and sidewalk. {reveal}
![Calcium chloride ice melt](image:snow-calcium-chloride)

---slide---
## Snow equipment
- Snow equipment, including the ATVs, is taught in person. {reveal}
- Do not run any snow equipment until your manager has trained you on it. {reveal}
