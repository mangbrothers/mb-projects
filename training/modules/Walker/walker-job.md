## Your job in one minute
- You hang Mangiapane Brothers door hangers on the front doors of homes on your routes. {reveal}
- You are paid for every hanger you hang. {reveal}
- Every hanger carries an offer with a deadline, so every route has a finish-by date. {reveal}
- Pass the quiz at the end of this module and the office gives you routes. {reveal}
![A door hanger distributor on a neighborhood sidewalk](image:job-overview)

---slide---
## Set your own hours, in daylight
- You choose your hours. We suggest 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. {reveal}
- Daylight only. Never hang after dark, not even to finish a street. {reveal}
- In November it gets dark around 5:15 p.m. Plan to be done by 5. {reveal}
- Whatever hours you pick, the route has to be done by its finish-by date. {reveal}
{highlight: 9 a.m. to 5 p.m., daylight only}
![Hang between 9 a.m. and 5 p.m., never after dark](image:hours-daylight)

---slide---
## Monday pickup
- Hangers are picked up every Monday for the week. {reveal}
- They come in stacks of 250, marked by pass, for example "Fall Cleanup Pass 1" or "Lights Pass 2". {reveal}
- Keep them dry and keep each pass separate. {reveal}
- Every hanger you did not hang comes back at the end of the week. {reveal}
- What you picked up minus what you return is your count. {reveal}
![Bundled stacks of door hangers, one pass per stack](image:hanger-pickup)

---slide---
## Before the first door
- Open the route on the site. It shows the pass, the start address and the streets. {reveal}
- Check that the hangers in your hand match the pass on the route. {reveal}
- Charge your phone, and bring the power bank the company gives you. {reveal}
- Start your Strava recording at the first door. {reveal}
![A route on the site: the pass, the start address and the streets](image:route-before-start)

---slide---
## On the knob first
- One hanger per house, on the front door. {reveal}
- Hang it on the doorknob or the handle first. {reveal}
- Pull it snug so it sits flat against the door. {reveal}
![A hanger on the front doorknob](image:hanger-on-knob)

---slide---
## Will not stay on the knob? Tuck it in the screen door
- Tuck the hanger into the screen door, at the handle or the sill, so the screen door holds it tight. {reveal}
- Never just set it against the door or on the step. {reveal}
- The wind takes it, and a hanger on the ground is litter with our name on it. {reveal}
- No knob it will stay on and no screen door: skip the house. {reveal}
{highlight: Tuck it in the screen door}
![A hanger tucked into a screen door](image:hanger-screen-door)

---slide---
## Five things you never do
- Never in or on a mailbox. That is against federal law. {reveal}
- Never on a house posted No Soliciting or No Trespassing. Skip it. {reveal}
- Never cut across a lawn or a bed. We maintain lawns for a living. {reveal}
- Never knock or ring the bell. {reveal}
- Never leave a hanger anywhere but that house's front door. {reveal}
![Mailbox, No Soliciting, lawn, doorbell: never](image:never-list)

---slide---
## Stay on the walk
- Stay on walkways, driveways and sidewalks. {reveal}
- Front doors only. You never need to open a gate. {reveal}
- Walk the streets in route order and finish each street. {reveal}
- Main roads: walk only the near side, the side with the rest of your route. {reveal}
![Walking the sidewalk, not the lawn](image:walk-the-sidewalk)

---slide---
## When a homeowner comes out
- You are not selling anything. Keep it short and friendly. {reveal}
- Say you are dropping off a flyer for Mangiapane Brothers, a local property maintenance company. {reveal}
- Questions or prices: point to the phone number on the hanger. Never quote a price. {reveal}
- If someone is upset, do not argue. Apologize and walk away. {reveal}
![A friendly hello at the door](image:homeowner-door)

---slide---
## Check off each street with a photo
- Finish the whole street, then check it off on the route. {reveal}
- Take one photo per street: a hung hanger with the street behind it. {reveal}
- Never check off a street you did not walk. {reveal}
![A hung hanger with the street behind it](image:street-photo)

---slide---
## What Strava is, and why we use it
- Strava is a free app that records a walk with your phone's GPS. It draws the path you walked on a map, with your distance and time. {reveal}
- It is your proof that the route was walked. The office lays your recording over the route map. {reveal}
- It protects you. If a homeowner says he never got a hanger, your recording shows you were on his street. {reveal}
- It only records while you have Record on. It does not follow you any other time. {reveal}
![Your recording laid over the route map](image:strava-what)

---slide---
## Setting up Strava
- Download the free Strava app and make an account. {reveal}
- Text your Strava username to 734-450-2400. The office adds it to your account. {reveal}
- The company sends you a follow request. Approve it. {reveal}
- Set your activities to Followers only: Settings, Privacy Controls, Activities. {reveal}
![Your Strava username on your account, added by the office](image:strava-setup)
note: Strava menu names change. Check this path on a phone before each hiring round.

---slide---
## Recording your walk
- At the first door, open Strava and tap Record. {reveal}
- Pick Walk, then tap Start. {reveal}
- At the last door, tap Stop, then Finish and Save. {reveal}
- Take a screenshot of the finished walk, with the map, the distance and the time showing. {reveal}
- That screenshot goes in your day report. {reveal}
![Record, Walk, Start at the first door. Stop, Finish, Save at the last](image:strava-record)

---slide---
## Your day report
- At the end of every day, send your day report on the site, at the bottom of Today. {reveal}
- Last address hung comes first, and it is required. {reveal}
- For each route: your count, your start and end time, and your Strava screenshot. {reveal}
- You can fix a report until the office closes the week. {reveal}
![The day report on the site](image:day-report)

---slide---
## How we check every route
- Every day report is checked. Not a sample: every one. {reveal}
- Your Strava recording is laid over the route map. Every street you checked off should be on it. {reveal}
- Your count is checked against the doors on those streets, and your time against a normal pace of about 80 doors an hour. {reveal}
- Your street photos are checked against the streets you checked off. {reveal}
- Within a few days, the office walks part of every route to see the hangers on the doors. {reveal}
- If something does not match, you hear from the office. A false count ends the agreement. {reveal}
![What the office sees: your count, your times, your Strava recording and your photos](image:office-check)

---slide---
## Rain, holidays and deadlines
- Never hang on Thanksgiving Day. {reveal}
- Hard rain: make the day up on Saturday. {reveal}
- The last snow pass moves with the first snowfall. You will be told the day before. {reveal}
- If you cannot finish a route by its date, call the office before the date, not after. {reveal}
![Rain days, Thanksgiving and finish-by dates](image:dates-weather)

---slide---
## What ends the agreement
- Missing two routes by their finish-by dates. {reveal}
- Dumping hangers. {reveal}
- Reporting a count you did not hang. {reveal}
- A hanger in a mailbox, or an argument with a homeowner. {reveal}
- Hangers that were not hung are not paid. {reveal}
![What ends the agreement](image:ends-agreement)
