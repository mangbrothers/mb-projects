## Checking a house off is your signature
- There is no checklist in the app. The checklist is this module. {reveal}
- When you check a house off, you confirm the checklist was done. {reveal}
- The owner spot-checks photos. Your check-off has to hold up. {reveal}

---slide---
## Walk it before the truck moves
- Walk the property after the crew finishes, every house. {reveal}
- Fix anything missed now. Coming back later costs the crew. {reveal}
- Then take one finished-job photo and check the house off. {reveal}
- Skipping the photo or the quality check ends employment. {reveal}

---slide---
## The photo
- One photo per house, after the work is finished. {reveal}
- Show the finished work: the lawn, the curb pile, the cleared drive, the lit roofline. {reveal}
- The photo is the record the work is paid on, and it may be sent to the client. {reveal}
- A stop with no photo is not paid. {reveal}
![A finished-job photo of a cleared lawn](image:finished-job-photo)

---slide---
## Lawn
- No obvious missed spots. {reveal}
- Edging done. {reveal}
- No clippings on the driveway, walks, sidewalk or anywhere on the property. {reveal}
- No clippings in the beds. {reveal}
- Heavy thatch blown off the lawn. {reveal}

---slide---
## Fall cleanup
- No leaves left on the lawn: front, sides and back. {reveal}
- All leaves at the curb in the street. Nothing on the sidewalk. {reveal}
- Driveway and mailbox clear of piles. {reveal}
- Walks and porch blown clean. Beds cleared as well as possible. {reveal}

---slide---
## Gutters
- Every run of gutter clear, and the downspout tops open. {reveal}
- Nothing left on the roof: tools, debris or the blower. {reveal}
- Gutter leaves in the yard cleared with the rest of the cleanup. {reveal}
- An overgrown gutter that was scooped by hand gets an incident report, type other, so it is billed. {reveal}
note: Overgrown gutters carry an extra charge on the fall cleanup. The office needs the report to bill it.

---slide---
## Lights
- Every bulb lit. Test before the truck moves. {reveal}
- Hardware green, black or bronze. Every empty socket capped. {reveal}
- Every cord joint covered. No cords across walkways. {reveal}
- Wreath hung, photocell timer at the outlet. {reveal}
- Nothing left on the roof or lawn. {reveal}

---slide---
## Snow
- Driveway to the back gate, the porch, the walk and the public sidewalk. {reveal}
- Snow on the sides of the driveway near the street. Never on a cleared walk or a neighbor's drive. {reveal}
- Ice melt down after clearing, on every visit. {reveal}

---slide---
## When the crew is behind
- Never check off a house that fails the checklist to save time. {reveal}
- Fix it before you move on, or tell the operations manager the route will run late. {reveal}
