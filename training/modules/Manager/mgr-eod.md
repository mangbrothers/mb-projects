---slide---
## Why the end of the day matters
- The equipment stays at your house until the company has a yard. {reveal}
- Fuel and checks happen at night, not in the morning. {reveal}
- That way the crew arrives at the meet spot ready to work, with no paid prep time. {reveal}

---slide---
## Equipment, every night
- Fuel every machine. Mixed fuel for the two-stroke equipment. {reveal}
- Blow off debris and check for damage. {reveal}
- Trimmer string loaded and edger blades good for the morning. {reveal}
- On snow, ice melt restocked on the truck. {reveal}
![Equipment fueled and loaded for the morning](image:eod-equipment-loaded)

---slide---
## The trailer
- Check the trailer before you drive home: hitch pin, chains, lights and tie-downs. {reveal}
- Park it locked, with the equipment secured. {reveal}
- Keys and route information stay with you, never with a crew member. {reveal}

---slide---
## Anything broken or used up
- Text the operations manager tonight about anything that needs a part, a repair or a replacement. {reveal}
- Same for any spare you used, so it is back on the truck. {reveal}
- Mower blades: swap weekly and hand the dull set to the operations manager. {reveal}

---slide---
## Approve the day
- Open Crew log once the last house is checked off. {reveal}
- Check every stop, every photo and everyone's clock-out. {reveal}
- Fix a missed clock-out time first. {reveal}
- Then tap Approve. It locks the day. {reveal}
note: How to use Crew log is in Using the site: crew managers.

---slide---
## Reports, the same day
- Every complaint, damage, skipped house and client question has an incident report before you approve. {reveal}
- Any injury on your crew goes to the owner the same day. {reveal}
- Someone did something worth the record, good or bad: write an employee report from Crew log. {reveal}

---slide---
## Do not approve what is wrong
- A house checked off without a photo is not paid. Fix it before you approve. {reveal}
- A clock-out that is wrong becomes a wrong paycheck. {reveal}
- If something does not add up, call the operations manager before you approve. {reveal}
