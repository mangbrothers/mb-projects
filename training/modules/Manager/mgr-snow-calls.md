## Who makes the call
- The operations manager tracks the storm, measures and makes the go or no-go call for every crew. {reveal}
- He decides on mid-storm pushes and sets the depth for each route. {reveal}
- You run your crew once the call is made. {reveal}

---slide---
## How the call works
- 1 inch or more: crews go out once the storm stops. {reveal}
- 5 inches or more: the operations manager may send crews mid-storm. A mid-storm trip is its own push, and the crew goes out again after the storm. {reveal}
- Every client is cleared within 24 hours of the storm ending. {reveal}

---slide---
## Your crew confirms
- Every alert needs "confirmed" from each man within 30 minutes. {reveal}
- Watch the group text. You confirm first. {reveal}
- Anyone who does not confirm gets a call from the operations manager. {reveal}
- If you cannot make it, tell the operations manager before the dispatch time. {reveal}

---slide---
## Texts you send
- Text the operations manager when the crew heads out. {reveal}
- Text him again when the route is finished. {reveal}
- Text him right away if the route will run late or a man drops out. {reveal}

---slide---
## Measuring on site
- Measure in a flat, open spot away from the house, the drive and any drift. {reveal}
- Push a ruler straight down to the ground and read it at eye level. {reveal}
- Drifting snow does not count toward the depth. {reveal}
- If what you measure is clearly off from the call, text the operations manager. He sets the depth. {reveal}
![Ruler pushed into snow in an open spot](image:snow-measure-ruler)

---slide---
## Hours
- No equipment before 7:00 a.m. or after 11:00 p.m. {reveal}
- Alerts may come overnight for a 7:00 a.m. start. Be up by 6:00 a.m. {reveal}
- Running equipment outside those hours ends employment. {reveal}

---slide---
## Running the route
- Route order as set in the system. Never skip ahead. {reveal}
- Clear first, then ice melt. Never ice melt before clearing. {reveal}
- Check each house off with one photo after the ice melt is down. {reveal}
- Leaving route order or salting before clearing ends employment. {reveal}

---slide---
## The ATV
- Helmet and eye protection every time you run the ATV. {reveal}
- Running the ATV without them ends employment. {reveal}
- The ATV is taught in person. No one runs it before training. {reveal}

---slide---
## City plow piles and markers
- Snow the city plow leaves at the apron after our visit is cleared on the next visit. {reveal}
- A client who asks for a return trip before then: log it as a client question. {reveal}
- Leave the drive markers where they are. Report any that are missing. {reveal}
