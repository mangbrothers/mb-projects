---slide---
## You are the person the client talks to
- Crew send clients to you for anything beyond a small request. {reveal}
- Be polite, calm and brief. Listen first. {reveal}
- You fix what you can on the spot. The office handles the rest. {reveal}

---slide---
## What you can do
- Redo work on the spot when the complaint is about our work. {reveal}
- Answer simple questions about today's visit. {reveal}
- Take down anything else and tell the client the office will follow up. {reveal}

---slide---
## What you never do
- Give a refund or a discount. {reveal}
- Quote a price or promise extra work. {reveal}
- Argue, or explain why it is not our fault. {reveal}
- Take work, cash or a side job from a client. That ends employment. {reveal}

---slide---
## A small complaint
- "You missed the side yard." Look, agree if it was missed, and fix it now. {reveal}
- Thank the client for telling you. {reveal}
- Log it on the site before the end of the day, even if it is fixed. {reveal}

---slide---
## A serious complaint
- Write down what the client says, in the client's words. {reveal}
- Take a photo of the problem. {reveal}
- Tell the client the office will follow up. Give no other promise. {reveal}
- File an incident report, type complaint, the same day. {reveal}

---slide---
## Damage
- Stop and look. Take a photo before anything is moved. {reveal}
- Tell the client plainly what happened, and that the office will follow up. {reveal}
- File an incident report, type damage, before the truck moves. {reveal}
- Never hide damage or leave it for the client to find. {reveal}
![Photo of damage for the report](image:damage-photo)

---slide---
## Could not service
- A locked gate, a loose dog, a car in the drive: tap Skip and say why. {reveal}
- File an incident report, type could not service, with a photo. {reveal}
- Do not wait at the house. Move to the next stop. {reveal}

---slide---
## Questions and requests
- A client asks about another service or a price: take a note. {reveal}
- File an incident report, type client question, so the office calls back. {reveal}
- Say "The office will call you about that." Nothing more. {reveal}

---slide---
## An angry client
- Stay calm. Do not raise your voice. {reveal}
- Say you will have the office call, then walk away. {reveal}
- If you feel unsafe, load the crew and leave. Report it the same day. {reveal}

---slide---
## Everything goes on the site
- Complaints, damage, skipped houses and questions are logged on the site, not by text. {reveal}
- Pick the right type: damage, complaint, could not service, client question or other. {reveal}
- The office sees damage and complaints first. {reveal}

### Q1
A client says the crew missed leaves along the side of the house. They did. What do you do?
- [ ] Offer a discount on the next visit
- [x] Have the crew fix it now, then log it on the site
- [ ] Tell the client the office will handle it
- [ ] Explain the side yard is hard to reach
explain: Redo the work on the spot when it is our miss, and log it the same day even after it is fixed.
slide: 4

### Q2
A client asks for money back because she is unhappy with the cleanup. What can you offer?
- [ ] A partial refund
- [ ] A free visit
- [x] To take down her complaint and have the office follow up
- [ ] A discount on snow removal
explain: Crew managers give no refunds or discounts. Write it down, photograph the problem and say the office will follow up.
slide: 5

### Q3
The blower kicks a stone into a client's window and cracks it. When do you file the report?
- [ ] At the end of the week
- [ ] Only if the client notices
- [x] Before the truck moves, type damage, with a photo
- [ ] After you call the client's insurance
explain: Photograph the damage, tell the client the office will follow up, and file a damage report before you leave.
slide: 6

### Q4
A client asks what you would charge to mulch her beds in the spring. What do you say?
- [ ] Give your best guess
- [ ] Offer to do it on a Saturday for cash
- [x] Say the office will call her, and file a client question report
- [ ] Tell her to look online
explain: Never quote a price or take side work. Log it as a client question so the office calls back.
slide: 8

### Q5
The side gate is locked and the backyard is part of the service. What do you do?
- [ ] Wait until the client comes home
- [ ] Climb the fence
- [x] Tap Skip with the reason and file a could not service report with a photo
- [ ] Do the front and check the house off
explain: A house that cannot be serviced gets a Skip with the reason and a could not service report. Then move on.
