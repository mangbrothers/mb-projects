## Your job in one line
- Get your crew through the route, in order, safely, with every house done right. {reveal}
- You run the crew. The operations manager runs the schedule. {reveal}
- How to use the site for each step is in Using the site: crew managers. {reveal}

---slide---
## Meet at a neutral spot
- Meet your crew at the spot the operations manager sets. Never outside a client's house. {reveal}
- The equipment arrives ready. It was fueled and checked the night before. {reveal}
- Everyone clocks in on his own phone. {reveal}
note: Draft timing is meet 7:45 a.m., brief for 10 minutes, at the first house by 8:00 a.m. Start times are not final; the operations manager sets them.

---slide---
## The morning brief
- Keep it to about 10 minutes. {reveal}
- Say where the route goes today and anything special about a house. {reveal}
- Give one safety point for today, in a sentence or two. {reveal}
- Make it specific: wet roofs, ice on the walks, traffic on a busy street. {reveal}

---slide---
## Check the trailer
- Check the trailer at the start and the end of every day, not every load. {reveal}
- Hitch pin in. Chains crossed and hooked. {reveal}
- Lights working. Every tie-down tight. {reveal}
![Trailer hitch with pin and chains](image:trailer-hitch-check)

---slide---
## Who does what in the truck
- Only you drive the truck and trailer. {reveal}
- The crew member in the passenger seat navigates to the next house on his own phone. {reveal}
- You are signed in on your phone. You do the quality check, take the photo and check the house off. {reveal}
- Your license must stay valid. Tell the company the same day if it is suspended. {reveal}

---slide---
## The slippery roof call is yours
- You make the call when a roof is too slippery to go on. {reveal}
- Wet, frosty or icy is always too slippery. No one goes up. {reveal}
- When you call it, nobody on the crew overrides you. {reveal}

---slide---
## Follow the route order
- The house order is set in the system. Follow it. {reveal}
- Do not reorder the route to suit the crew. {reveal}
- If a house cannot be done, tap Skip and say why, then move on. {reveal}
- On snow, leaving route order ends employment. {reveal}

---slide---
## Phones and conduct on the job
- No phones during a job. In the truck between jobs is fine. {reveal}
- When the truck stops, phones away and everyone out ready to work. {reveal}
- You enforce the handbook. If you let it slide, the crew learns it does not count. {reveal}

---slide---
## Lunch
- You call lunch. It can run up to an hour. {reveal}
- The crew stays together. No driving off, and no personal use of the truck. {reveal}
- Lunch is the crew's time. Nobody is required to work through it. {reveal}

---slide---
## When someone does not show
- Attendance belongs to the operations manager, not you. {reveal}
- Call the operations manager as soon as you know someone is missing. {reveal}
- Run the route with who you have. Do not wait at the meet spot. {reveal}

---slide---
## When someone gets hurt
- Life threatening, like a roof fall, a head injury or heavy bleeding: call 911. {reveal}
- Serious but not life threatening: get him to the hospital first. A crew member calls Russel on the way. {reveal}
- Minor: urgent care. {reveal}
- Report any injury on your crew to the owner the same day. {reveal}
note: The nearest hospital is in the Safety module: Trinity Health Livonia Hospital, 36475 Five Mile Rd at Levan.

---slide---
## End the day right
- Last house checked off, with its photo. {reveal}
- Check the trailer before you drive home. {reveal}
- Equipment and reporting are in End-of-day equipment and reporting. {reveal}
