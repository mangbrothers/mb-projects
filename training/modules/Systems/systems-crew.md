## Signing in
- Open **apps.mangbrothers.com**
- Tap **Sign in with Google**
- Use the Google account the office has on file for you
- You go straight to the Crews page
{highlight: Sign in with Google}
![The sign-in page](image:crew-signin)
---slide---
## Not on the list?
- The page says your email is not on the team list: you used a different Google account
- Tap **Sign out and use another account**
- It says you are not on a crew yet: the office puts you on one
- Still stuck: tell your crew manager or call the office at 734-450-2400
![Not on a crew yet](image:crew-not-on-crew)

## Start here: four steps before your first shift
- Until you finish this deck, **Training** is the only tab you see {reveal}
- Step 2: sign your offer, then the rest of your paperwork, from the cards at the top of Training {reveal}
- Step 3: pass the safety and conduct quizzes {reveal}
- Step 4: the office puts you on the schedule. **Before you can be scheduled** shows what is left {reveal}
![Day one: only the Training tab, with this deck first](image:crew-training-first)
---slide---
## Your tabs
- **Schedule**: your shifts, meetings and storm call-outs for the next month
- **Training**: modules and paperwork
- **Hours**: your time by pay period
- **Pay**: what you earned and what payroll paid
- **Files** and **My info**: your papers and your contact details
![Your tabs](image:crew-tabs)
---slide---
## Schedule
- **Clock in** at the top when you start, **Clock out** when you finish
- Shifts for the next month, with the start time and any note
- Trainings and meetings show on the same list
- Storm days show once the office starts a storm
![The Schedule tab](image:crew-schedule)
---slide---
## Hours and Pay
- **Hours** lists your time, two weeks at a time
- Your manager approves each day; approved hours are locked
- **Pay** shows what you earned and what was paid
- Something looks wrong: tell your crew manager the same week
![Hours, two weeks at a time](image:crew-hours)
---slide---
## My info
- Keep your phone, address and emergency contact up to date
- Your crew manager sees them so he can reach you
- Pay details and tax forms are never shown to your crew manager
![My info](image:crew-info)
---slide---
## Your phone on the job
- The passenger navigates to the next house on his own phone
- The address is on the route; your crew manager runs the check-offs
- No phone use during a job; in the truck between jobs is fine
![Phones: in the truck yes, on a property no](image:crew-phone)
