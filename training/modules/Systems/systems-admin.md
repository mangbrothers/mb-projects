## One sign-in, every page
- Sign in at **apps.mangbrothers.com** with your Google account
- Admins land on **Projects**
- The menu at the top left opens every page
---slide---
## The admin pages
- **Projects**: the admin task dashboard
- **CRM**: leads, clients, contracts, billing and the service log
- **Team Manager**: hiring, personnel, crews, paperwork, training, reports, payroll
- **Previews**: what customers and crews see, with sample data
---slide---
## The employee pages
- **Crews**: the day, routes, schedules, the log and meetings
- **Routes**: door hanger routes, seats, review and setup
- **See as** on either page shows exactly what one person sees; nothing is saved
---slide---
## Training in Team Manager
- **Import the Training folder** brings in modules written to the spec
- Assign material to a role or to one person
- **Progress** shows who is ready for the schedule
- A person's page shows his quiz tries, his gates and manager training
---slide---
## The gates
- Crew get shifts only after the offer, the safety and conduct quizzes and the crew systems deck
- Managers also need the manager deck and the practice run
- Door hangers get a seat only after the agreement, the job quiz and the door hanger deck
- A module not imported yet is waived until it is
---slide---
## Where the office checks the day
- Crews, **Log**: every crew's day, photos, incident reports, approvals
- Routes, **Review**: door hanger reports and photos
- Damage and complaints in incident reports come first
