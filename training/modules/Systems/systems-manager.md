## What a crew manager runs on the site
- The day's route and the map {reveal}
- Checking off each house, with one photo {reveal}
- Incident reports {reveal}
- Approving your crew's day in **Crew log** {reveal}
![A crew manager's Today](image:mgr-overview)
---slide---
## Your tabs
- **Today**: your clock, the day's routes and meetings
- **Week**: what is coming up
- **Crew log**: everything your crew did today, and the approve button
- **Crew**: your crew's phones, emergency contacts and shifts
- **Training** and **Me**: your training, schedule, hours and pay
![Week: what is coming up](image:mgr-week)
---slide---
## Clocking in
- Tap **Clock in** on Today when the day starts
- Tap **Clock out** when it ends
- Everyone on the crew clocks themselves in on their own phone
![Clock in at the top of Today](image:mgr-clock)
---slide---
## The route
- Tap a route card to open the map and the stop list
- Stops are in order; follow the order set in the system
- **Directions** opens your phone's maps app
- The yard address is at the top of the route
![A route with its stops](image:mgr-route)
---slide---
## Checking off a house
- Do the quality check first, then tap the box to check the house off {reveal}
- Checking it off means you confirm the checklist was done {reveal}
- Add one finished-job photo with **Add photo** {reveal}
- A stop with no photo is not paid {reveal}
{highlight: Add photo}
![The stop list under the map](image:mgr-checkoff)
---slide---
## Lights stops
- A lights stop asks what was done
- Installed, takedown, or a service call, with or without a ladder
- Each one bills differently, so pick the right one
![What was done on a lights stop](image:mgr-lights)
---slide---
## Skip and Note
- Could not do a house: tap **Skip** and say why
- A skipped house bills nothing
- **Note** adds a short note to a house you checked off
![Skipping a house](image:mgr-skip)
---slide---
## Incident reports
- Tap **Report** on the house
- Pick the type: damage, complaint, could not service, client question or other
- Write what happened and add a photo if it helps
- The office sees it right away; damage and complaints come first
{highlight: Report}
![The incident report](image:mgr-incident)
---slide---
## Snow days
- When the operations manager calls a storm, your snow route shows on Today
- Check off each house as you go, same as any route
- No equipment before 7 a.m. or after 11 p.m.
![A storm day on Today](image:mgr-storm)
---slide---
## Approving the day
- Open **Crew log** at the end of the day {reveal}
- Check every stop, every photo and everyone's clock-out {reveal}
- Fix a missed clock-out time first {reveal}
- Tap **Approve**: it locks the day, approves the hours and sends the stops to billing {reveal}
![The crew log](image:mgr-crewlog)
---slide---
## Employee reports
- Optional, from Crew log
- Write one when someone did something worth the record, good or bad
- It goes to the office and his file; he does not see it
![Writing an employee report](image:mgr-report)
---slide---
## Your practice run
- Before you can be made a manager, you run one practice day
- Training tab: **Start the practice run**
- Check off a house, add a photo, file an incident report and approve the day
- Nothing in practice reaches a client, billing or payroll
![The practice run card on Training](image:mgr-practice)
